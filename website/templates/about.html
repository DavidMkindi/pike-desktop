About

A quieter way to work with AI.

Pike started as a simple question: what if the AI app got out of the way and let you use the models you already have?

Why Pike exists

Most AI chat apps want to be your only provider, your only account and your only place for data. Pike takes the opposite approach. It is a thin, local-first client that talks to any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, keeps your keys and history on your own disk, and gets out of the way once you are working.

What that means in practice

  • You own the key. No proxy accounts, no rate limits imposed by us.
  • You own the history. A local SQLite file, searchable and deletable.
  • You pick the model. Swap providers per conversation without losing anything.
  • You stay in control. Workspace tools only act on the folder you opened.

One product, several surfaces

The same design tokens and behaviour power three things: the desktop app, the browser mode and the VS Code extension. Learn Pike once and it feels familiar everywhere.

Free, and staying that way

Pike is free to use and modify. You pay only your provider for the tokens you use - there is no Pike subscription and no markup on top.

Built with care. Pike is a small project made by someone who wanted a better thinking partner. Enjoy yours.
